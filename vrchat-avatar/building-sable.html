Before you start

The one thing that will actually block you

Every tool below is genuinely free. The blocker isn't money — it's your VRChat account. Brand-new Visitor accounts cannot upload avatars. You need to reach New User trust rank first, which means verifying your email and spending some hours actually playing. Start that clock today, because it runs in the background while you build everything else.

Budget roughly three evenings: one to design her, one to fight Unity into place, one to convert, polish and upload. The middle evening is the annoying one. Everything after it is fast.

Sable

A design brief, not a rule. These are concrete values you can type straight into the color pickers — change any of them and she's more yours, which is the point.

Hair
Deep charcoal-plum at the root melting to soft lilac at the tips. VRoid does root-to-tip gradients natively, so this costs you two color pickers and nothing else. Medium length, heavy fringe, slightly overgrown.
Eyes
Warm honey-amber with a darker outer ring. This is the only warm color on her — against all that cool plum it does a lot of work. Large iris, low-set highlight.
Ears
Charcoal outer to match the hair root, dusty rose inner. Set them slightly further back and wider apart than the preset default — dead-center-on-top reads as a headband, off-center reads as anatomy.
Face
The signature: a light dusting of freckles across the nose bridge and upper cheeks, plus soft under-eye blush. Two minutes in the face-paint layer, and it's the thing people remember.
Outfit
Oversized sage knit sweater, worn falling off one shoulder. Black shorts underneath, mostly hidden. Sleeves past the hands.
Legs
Mismatched thigh-highs — one charcoal, one lilac. The deliberate asymmetry. It's small, it's free, and it's the difference between a preset and a character.
Tail
Charcoal with a lilac tip, echoing the hair gradient. VRoid ships a cat tail accessory as of v1.27.0, so this is a dropdown, not a modeling job.
